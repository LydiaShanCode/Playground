Room Visuals

Visuals that listen to the music in the room and follow its tempo, key, genre, rhythm and melodic phrasing.

Try the demo band
  • BPM sets the speed. Slower music moves slower, with longer trails and softer pulses.
  • Key sets the colours, walking the circle of fifths. Minor keys are darker and cooler.
  • Genre picks the scene: tunnels, shards, orbits or a drifting particle field.
  • Rhythm: kicks send shockwaves, snares flash, hi-hats sparkle, the downbeat zooms.
  • Melodic phrasing: a ribbon traces the melody, then blooms if the phrase comes home to the key.
